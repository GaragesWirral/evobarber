# Keyword Research: EVO Private Barber Menorca

Luxury private barber who travels to the client anywhere on the island (hotels, villas, residences, yachts). Languages: Spanish, English, French. Segments: locals, tourists in villas and hotels, yacht clients, weddings and events.

Volumes are qualitative tiers (High / Medium / Low) based on autocomplete signals, competitor targeting (Otero Barber Marbella, Barber Traveler, international mobile barbers) and market research. Validate with Google Keyword Planner once Google Ads is set up.

## Primary keyword

**barbero a domicilio en menorca** (homepage H1 and title). This mirrors the exact pattern Otero Barber ranks with in Marbella ("Barbero a domicilio en Marbella"). Low competition in Menorca: no dedicated luxury mobile barber currently targets it.

## Seasonality

Local demand (ES) is year round. Tourist, yacht and wedding clusters peak May to October, matching the Menorca season. Weddings peak June and September. Plan content refresh and Google Business Profile posts in March and April before the season.
