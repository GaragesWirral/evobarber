The full ritual

Eleven moments, one experience

Every service is built to measure, combining these elements according to your needs and preferences.

  1. 01

    Face shape analysis and consultation

    A personalised study of your face and style to recommend the look that best enhances your image.

  2. 02

    Tailored haircut

    Advanced techniques and impeccable finishes for an elegant, natural result.

  3. 03

    Beard ritual

    Hot towel, lather applied with a brush, straight razor shave and precision outlining.

  4. 04

    Relaxing facial massage

    With a high quality moisturising cream to revitalise, reduce tension and improve the appearance of the skin.

  5. 05

    Facial gua sha

    An ancient technique that stimulates circulation, reduces inflammation and adds radiance.

  6. 06

    Under eye patches

    Decongests, hydrates and refreshes the eye area during the service.

  7. 07

    Scalp massage

    Encourages deep relaxation, relieves tension and supports overall wellbeing.

  8. 08

    Aromatherapy

    Selected essences to create an exclusive atmosphere and enhance the feeling of wellbeing.

  9. 09

    Personalised music

    A musical selection adapted to the client's taste.

  10. 10

    Selected professional products

    Only high end products that care for your hair, beard and skin, guaranteeing the best results.

  11. 11

    Final consultation

    Styling advice and a personalised routine to keep the perfect look every day.
