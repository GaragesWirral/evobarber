What having a mobile barber really means

A mobile barber is much more than a haircut at home. It means having a trusted professional who knows your style, your hair type and your preferences, and who comes to you whenever you need him. No travelling, no waiting rooms and no sharing the moment with anyone else.

At EVO Private Barber every service starts with a face shape consultation to recommend the look that best enhances your image. From there we build a routine around you: a tailored haircut, a beard ritual with hot towel and straight razor shave, facial treatments and final styling advice so you can keep the result perfect every day.

For residents and professionals in Menorca

Many of my clients are residents who prefer a recurring service at home or at the office. If you value your time, I can suggest a fixed schedule so your image is always impeccable without you having to think about it. Punctuality is part of the commitment: I arrive at the agreed time with everything prepared and sterilised.

My training in men's barbering includes London, one of the international reference cities of the industry, complemented with specialised training in face shape analysis and image consulting. Continuous education is a fundamental part of my work.

Complete autonomy

A full barbershop that travels with you

I travel with a fully equipped portable workstation: a mirror with professional lighting, sterilised tools and carefully selected products. That lets me deliver a true barbershop experience with complete autonomy, in any room of your home, on your terrace or in your garden.

You choose the place and the music. I take care of the rest, including aromatherapy to create an exclusive atmosphere and enhance the feeling of wellbeing.
