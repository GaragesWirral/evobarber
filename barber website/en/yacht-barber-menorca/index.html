Private barbering on your vessel

Mahon Harbour is one of the most spectacular natural ports in the Mediterranean and every summer it welcomes yachts from all over the world. If you are spending a few days sailing the waters of Menorca, there is no need to look for a barbershop ashore or bend your plans around commercial opening hours. The EVO service comes to your berth.

I work with a portable station with a lit mirror and complete autonomy, designed to adapt to the deck, the saloon or any space on board. The equipment arrives sterilised and the products are carefully selected, with particular attention to hair and skin exposed to sun and salt.

For owners, guests and crew

I serve owners and their guests as well as full crews. For crews I organise consecutive turns and adapt to the boat's schedule, something captains especially appreciate during charter season, when the team's presentation is part of the service.

Discretion is the rule. Many of my clients on board value exactly that: a punctual professional, quiet about what he sees and focused on the result.

How it works

From the dock to your deck in minutes

You tell me the marina, the vessel name and the time that suits you. I arrive at the berth with all the equipment, set up the station wherever you prefer and within minutes the experience begins: a tailored haircut, a hot towel beard ritual and a straight razor shave if you wish.

If you are at anchor, we can coordinate a pick up at the dock with your tender. Everything is arranged with a simple WhatsApp message.
