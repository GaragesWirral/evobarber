The comfort of staying exactly where you are

Menorca is made for switching off, and losing a morning looking for a barbershop in a place you do not know is not part of the plan. With EVO the service comes to you: I travel to hotels, villas, country hotels and apartments across the island with a fully equipped portable barber station, lit mirror included.

The result is an experience many clients describe as the highlight of their holiday: a tailored haircut or a hot towel beard ritual on the terrace of your villa, by the pool or in the garden, with aromatherapy and the music you choose.

Perfect before a dinner or a celebration

Dinner in Ciutadella's old port, a family celebration, a photo session on the beach or simply the wish to look your best. Whatever the occasion, I arrive at the agreed time, absolutely punctual, and in under an hour you are ready.

If you travel with a group or family, I can serve several people in the same visit with consecutive turns. Ideal for groups of friends, celebrations and families with children who prefer not to wait at a barbershop.

The whole island

From Punta Prima to Cala Morell

I work anywhere in Menorca: Mahon, Ciutadella, Alaior, Es Castell, Sant Lluis, Es Mercadal, Ferreries and every coastal resort, from Binibeca to Son Bou, from Cala Galdana to Coves Noves and Fornells.

Hotel concierges and private villa managers can book directly on behalf of their guests. If you manage accommodation on the island and would like to offer this service to your clients, let's talk.
