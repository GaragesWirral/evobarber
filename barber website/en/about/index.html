Training and experience

From London to Menorca

I’m Luka Folch, founder of EVO Private Barber Menorca.

My career in barbering and personal grooming took me to London, where I continued my training at Sassoon Academy and TONI&GUY Academy, two internationally renowned hairdressing academies. That experience shaped my approach to cutting, technique and, above all, the importance of offering a completely personalised service.

Menorca is part of my story and part of the essence of EVO.
