Who I am

Your private barber, wherever you are

I am Luka Folch, founder of EVO Private Barber Menorca. I created EVO to offer a private, exclusive barber service for clients who value comfort, discretion and highly personalised attention.

My philosophy is built on discretion, punctuality, care for detail and creating a relaxed, professional wellbeing experience. Every service adapts to the needs and preferences of each client.
