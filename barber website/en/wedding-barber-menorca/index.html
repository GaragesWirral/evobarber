The groom's ritual, without leaving the venue

The morning of a wedding is full of important moments and time flies. With EVO you do not have to travel to a barbershop or depend on anyone else's schedule. I arrive at the place where you are getting ready with my fully equipped portable station and turn a quiet corner into a private barbershop.

The groom's service includes a face shape consultation, a tailored haircut with impeccable finishes and the complete beard ritual: hot towel, brush applied lather, straight razor shave and precision outlining. If you wish, we add a facial massage, under eye patches and a scalp massage so you reach the ceremony relaxed and with perfect skin.

Also for best men, witnesses and guests

Many couples book the service for the whole group. I organise the turns in advance so everyone has their moment without rushing and without interfering with the rest of the preparations. It is an experience the group genuinely enjoys and it looks beautiful in the getting ready photos.

Menorca is one of the most beautiful wedding destinations in the Mediterranean and I work across the entire island: Ciutadella, Mahon, inland estates, seaside villas and boutique hotels. If your wedding is in Menorca, your barber is already here.

How it works

Planned to the minute for your big day

Before the wedding we talk about the style you want, the number of people and the ceremony time. With that information I prepare a timing plan and arrive with plenty of margin so everything flows without stress.

Punctuality is part of my commitment. The equipment arrives sterilised, the products are carefully selected and the result is designed to stay perfect from the ceremony to the last dance.
